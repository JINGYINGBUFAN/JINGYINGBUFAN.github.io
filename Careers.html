CONNECT THE WORLD

Civil Avitation

Civil aviation is an important bridge in connecting people, companies, and cultures. It provides fast and easy travel and thus fuels tourism, trade, and cross-cultural exchange. The industry has played a really important role in uniting the world, whether it be through facilitating international commerce or reuniting families.

A dynamic innovation field, based on the principles of safety, efficiency, and sustainability—it is civil aviation. This industry has seen incredible change over the years, driven by impressive technological advances. A role in this fast-moving organization is an opportunity to help shape the future of global connectivity.

THE UNIVERISE

Space Exploration

Just like in the field of aviation, space exploration is one exciting frontier wherein everybody with diversified skills and interests can be a participant. Companies like CORBUS design and construct spacecraft, which take people from Earth to explore other planets or to improve communication through satellites. In this field, there is a place for everybody: engineering, science, technology, management.

Space exploration is all about overcoming big problems and creating new technologies. It's a mission that pulls together people from all over the world to do something bigger than ourselves. If you want to learn about the universe and help create a better future, then space exploration provides great chances for growth and adventure.

DEFENCE

MILITARY AIRCRAFTS

CORBUS Military Aircraft business unit is committed to developing and manufacturing aircraft to aid global defense and security. We use our aircraft as part of transport troops, surveillance and for peacekeeping efforts. Our mission is to develop advanced, reliable and efficient planes in order to fulfil the needs of today's defence operations.

If you are passionate and experienced in military aviation we want you to join our team and help mould the future of military aviation. If you are an engineer, you're a technician, you have expertise in other areas, there is something exciting you can do in global security. CORBUS Military Aircraft is looking for an innovative team to drive the future of defense technology and you can be part of it.

SAVE LIFES

RESCUE AIRCRAFTS

CORBUS Rescue Aircraft department designs and builds of aircraft that are vital for emergency response and disaster relief. Rescue operations, humanitarian missions and helping bringing essential aid to places effected by natural disasters and crisis – these are the tasks these aircrafts are used for. Our rescue aircraft features advanced technology on a true rescue aircraft, designed and built for operation in challenging and often critical environments, saving lives.

And so we are seeking talented people to join our team to help on this important work. Whatever your calling — engineer, technician or someone who simply loves to help people — there are a lot of exciting opportunities to be on a team that makes a difference. Your support helps provide Airbus Rescue Aircraft when it’s needed most — join us.
