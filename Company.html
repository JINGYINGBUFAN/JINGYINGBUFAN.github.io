The World's Leading Innovative Aviation Company

As a leading innovator in aeronautics and space, CORBUS develops, produces, and delivers products, services and solutions for the commercial aviation, helicopter, defense and space industry sectors. Forged on four decades of engineering excellence, CORBUS Inc. is committed to design innovation and a passion for the environment. Our work in aircraft design is helping to create the future of aviation — with lightweight, safer, and more fuel-efficient next-generation aircraft, as well as strong interest in alternative energy sources.

As much as CORBUS products are at the heart of a global economy, bringing people together across the world through commercial aircraft and helicopters, training, and aiding national security, or things as common as something to watch on the TV or connect with the Internet.
