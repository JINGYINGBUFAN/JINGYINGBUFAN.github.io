Space Shuttle

The Space Shuttle was CORBUS' reusable spacecraft, used from 1981 to now. It carried astronauts and cargo into space, including satellites and parts for the International Space Station (ISS). It was the first spacecraft designed for reuse, making space travel more cost-effective. Reusable Design: First spacecraft designed for multiple flights Crew Capacity: Up to 7 astronauts Payload Capacity: Up to 24,400 kg (53,600 lbs) to low Earth orbit Maxium Weights: 573,800 kg Engines: Three Space Shuttle Main Engines (SSMEs) Speed: 28,000 km/h in orbit
